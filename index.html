The fine print: these are estimates to help you decide, not a guarantee of what you'll earn, and not tax or financial advice. Mile Money is an independent tool — it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to DoorDash, Uber, Uber Eats, or Instacart. Those names are used only to describe which apps this works with.

At tax time: nothing is withheld from this money. Set aside roughly a quarter of it and write down your odometer. Business miles are deductible at 72.5¢ through June 30, 2026 and 76¢ from July 1 on — keep the two halves of the year separate. Deduct every mile, not just the paid ones: driving to the zone, between orders, and home again all count.
